Regiment events

Monday Night Madness and more.

The 2nd Coalition Monday Night Madness event began on 05/01/2026. Built with help from Maj. Duo, it was designed to keep the night fun, structured, and easy to follow.

Event information

What the event is about

Extra focus was put into hard coding the rules as much as possible, while keeping the remaining rules simple and easy to follow. The goal is to give everyone a good time and keep rivalries from spilling over into the event.

We know Holdfast has its share of regiment rivalries, but we expect that baggage to be left at the door.

Event features

  • Unique army battlefield lobby with an accented map.
  • Every event ends in a fun round with something new each time.
  • Unique line battle rotations with map tweaks in development.

How it is run

The event has clear guidelines for admins so everyone knows what to expect. The focus is on fair balance, engaging game play, and an event where you can blaze your own trail without fear of griefing.
