Requirements

Read these before joining. The rules are simple, but they matter.

Respect & Conduct

Treat all members with respect. No harassment, hate speech, slurs, excessive toxicity, spamming, trolling, or deliberate disruption.

Chain of Command

Follow orders from officers and NCOs during events. Respect the regimental hierarchy in training, line battles, and operations.

Voice & Chat Etiquette

Keep comms clear during events. Unnecessary chatter disrupts coordination. Use push-to-talk if you have background noise.

Content Guidelines

No NSFW content, including porn, gore, or shock material. Avoid heated political or religious debates. No advertising other servers or clans without permission.

Representation

You represent 2C in-game and beyond, so act respectfully and follow the rules. No teamkilling, griefing, or breaking public server rules.

Enforcement & Common Sense

Moderators and officers may act at their discretion. If you think it might break the rules, don't do it.
